# Ludo

A small local multiplayer Ludo game that runs in a browser. It has no install step and does not need an internet connection.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. Choose 2, 3, or 4 players, then take turns on the same screen.

- Roll a 6 to bring a piece out of your yard.
- Click or focus a highlighted piece to move it.
- Land on an unsafe square to send rival pieces there back to their yards.
- Roll a 6 for another turn.
- Bring all four of your pieces home to win. You need an exact roll to finish.

Use **New game** to reset the current player setup.
